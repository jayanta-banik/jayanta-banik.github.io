Jayanta Banik

I am a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer building practical, evaluable ML systems across large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, genomic language modeling, and agentic AI.

Most recently at Aidar Health, I built HIPAA-compliant clinical NLP and AI pipelines over ~11M notes. Much of my work centers on AI security, guardrails, and hallucination reduction for healthcare and biomedical applications, spanning retrieval-augmented generation and knowledge graphs for real-time clinical decision support. I am also deeply interested in genomic data science and machine learning, from DNA language modeling to sequence-level prediction. I was fortunate to do this work under the mentorship of Dr. Gene Fridman (Johns Hopkins School of Medicine Faculty).

My broader interests include remote sensing and robotics for healthcare and precision agriculture, bringing sensing, learning, and automation together in the field and the clinic.

I hold a Master's in Computer Engineering from the University of California, Riverside, and a Bachelor's in Computer Engineering from West Bengal University of Technology. Outside of work I'm an astrophotography enthusiast.

Selected Projects

DNA BERT 2026 - present
A custom DNA language model using k-mer tokenization and masked-language modeling, pretrained with PySpark over a 64B-datapoint DNA/protein corpus. Reaches 95% accuracy on antimicrobial-resistance gene classification.
Memory Cycle for Agentic AI 2026
An MCP server for GitHub Copilot with spec-driven agents, hybrid RAG + knowledge-graph retrieval via PageIndex, a LangChain/LangGraph data-masking security layer, and a TurboVec validation layer to mitigate prompt poisoning.
FAMNet: Agentic AI Framework 2023
A Mixture-of-Experts architecture matching larger-LLM performance on GLUE at T5-small scale, with a fine-tuned BERT router and in-memory vector cache for fast, accurate tool selection.
Sign Language Translation 2023
A production API integrating video processing and NLP that converts sequences of sign language into natural spoken language, released as an open-source model on Hugging Face.
Emotional AI 2022
A hybrid model pairing a BERT multi-label emotion classifier with a BART generator for overlapping-emotion detection and emotional text generation.

Professional Experience

Clinical NLP Pipeline
Data Scientist · Aidar Health · 2025-2026

A clinical natural-language pipeline that reads around 11 million unstructured clinical notes a day and turns them into structured, queryable data the care team can act on. It masks personal information, maps free text to standard ICD-10 codes, and stays in near real-time sync with source records, making narrative notes that were previously unusable a reliable basis for downstream models and clinical decisions.

Real-Time Clinical RAG + Knowledge Graph
Data Scientist · Aidar Health · 2025-2026

A real-time system that grounds a language model's recommendations for nurses in each patient's full history rather than a single snapshot. Retrieval-augmented generation is paired with a knowledge graph of medications, interactions, and antibiotic-resistance patterns, so guidance stays anchored in real context. Care response time dropped by about 75%, cutting roughly 15 hours of turnaround.

Real-Time Signal-Processing Engine
Data Scientist · Aidar Health · 2025-2026

A redesign of the engine that processes patients' vital-sign streams in near real time. Profiling the bottlenecks, streamlining the core workflow, and moving the heaviest paths to C++ brought processing time from 45 seconds down to 3, about 15 times faster, with automated checks in the release pipeline to keep it there.

Longitudinal Observational Studies & Hospitalization Risk
Data Scientist · Aidar Health · 2025-2026

A set of observational studies following patients over time to understand how medications, vital-sign changes, and health deterioration relate to hospitalization risk and recovery. The resulting early-warning models flagged at-risk patients across 52 facilities and contributed to preventing 12 hospitalizations.

Patient-Journey Digital Twin
Data Scientist · Aidar Health · 2025-2026

A live digital twin of each patient's journey through remote-sensing care. It gave clinical and operations teams an up-to-date picture of where a patient was in their treatment, and a way to weigh interventions before they were needed.

Sensor Directed Sampling
Graduate Student Researcher · USDA ARS Salinity Lab · 2022-2024

A Sensor-Directed Sampling algorithm (the basis of the thesis) that decides where to sample next in orchards and fields to calibrate and validate remote-sensing instruments. By treating sub-sampling as an experimental-design problem, it extracts the most information from the fewest measurements, improving sub-sampling efficiency by about 28%.

Active Learning for Multi-Sensor Remote Sensing
Graduate Student Researcher · USDA ARS Salinity Lab · 2022-2024

An active-learning system that reduces the labeled data multi-sensor remote-sensing models need by about 65%. A pattern-based design with pluggable sensors and acquisition policies lets it keep requesting only the most informative samples instead of labeling everything.

Spatial Time-Series Analysis
Graduate Student Researcher · USDA ARS Salinity Lab · 2022-2024

A spatial time-series model and sensor-driven sampling pipeline for field-scale crop monitoring. Careful feature engineering and sampling design held spatial coverage accuracy to at least 94% while cutting field-sampling cost by about $64,000 a year.
